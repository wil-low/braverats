# Braverats — Game Rules

2 players · ~5 min · First to 4 rounds wins · Simultaneous card selection

---

## Story

Two rival princes vie for the throne: Blue (calm, disciplined) and Red (passionate, bold). Each harbors a secret love for the other's sister. Who will prevail?

---

## Setup

Each player receives 8 cards of one color (Red/Blue — effects are identical): Jester, Lady, Spy, Assassin, Chancellor, Magician, General, Prince.

---

## Play

1. Both players secretly select one card.
2. Reveal simultaneously. Powers resolve (all mandatory), then compare strength.
3. Winner takes the round; used cards stay face-up (loser's turned sideways).
4. Repeat until someone wins 4 rounds or cards run out.

---

## Draws & "On Hold"

If a round is drawn (including Jester vs. anything):
- The round is **on hold**.
- The winner of the **next decisive** round wins that round *and* all held rounds.
- Multiple rounds can accumulate.

---

## Card Effects

| Card | Power |
|------|-------|
| **Jester (0)** | Round nullified → on hold. Overrides all powers except Magician. |
| **Lady (1)** | If opponent played Prince, you win the game instantly. |
| **Spy (2)** | Next round, opponent reveals first. Cancels if both play Spy. |
| **Assassin (3)** | Lowest strength wins (inverts comparison). Applies after other effects. |
| **Chancellor (4)** | If you win with this card, it counts as 2 points. Stacks with held rounds. Cancels if both play it. |
| **Magician (5)** | Nullifies opponent's power — only strength compared. Does not affect General's delayed +2. |
| **General (6)** | Your next card gets +2 strength. Cannot be nullified by a subsequent Magician. |
| **Prince (7)** | Wins the round regardless of strength. Assassin doesn't override this. Jester still nullifies. Both Princes → power cancels, compare strength normally. |

---

## Game End

The game ends immediately when:
- A player reaches **4 points** (including bonus points from held rounds + Chancellor), or
- **Lady** defeats **Prince** (instant win).

If all cards are used and no one reaches 4 points → **draw**.
